## BRAINTEASER

**BRAINTEASER** — *BRinging Artificial INTelligencE home for a better cAre of amyotrophic lateral sclerosis and multiple SclERosis* — is a research project funded under **Horizon 2020**.

The project focuses on the use of artificial intelligence and data-driven approaches to support the care and management of amyotrophic lateral sclerosis (ALS) and multiple sclerosis.
