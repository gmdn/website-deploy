## MICOLL

**MICOLL** — *Migrating Commercial Law and Language: Rethinking lex mercatoria (11th–17th century)* — is a research project funded under the **Horizon 2020 European Research Council (ERC)** programme.

The project investigates the historical development, circulation, and transformation of commercial law and legal language across Europe between the 11th and 17th centuries.
