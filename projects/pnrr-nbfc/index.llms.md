## National Biodiversity Future Center

The **National Biodiversity Future Center (NBFC)** is Italy’s national research center dedicated to biodiversity and is funded within the **National Recovery and Resilience Plan (PNRR)** under the **NextGenerationEU** framework.

I participate in **Spoke 1**, which aims to establish an integrated national system for the observation and monitoring of marine biodiversity.

### Project information

- **Project:** National Biodiversity Future Center (NBFC)
- **Programme:** National Recovery and Resilience Plan (PNRR)
- **Funding:** NextGenerationEU
- **Duration:** 2022–2026
- **Role:** Participant in Spoke 1
- **Focus:** Integrated national system for marine biodiversity observation
